About Us

Kuniya Learning Management System is designed to make education simple, accessible, and effective through digital technology. The platform provides a centralized environment where students can access learning resources and continue their studies from anywhere.

The system supports different types of users, including Administrators, Instructors, and Students. Administrators can manage users, courses, study materials, and academic information. Instructors can provide learning content and support students, while students can access courses, study materials, assignments, and assessments.

Kuniya Learning Management System provides a convenient way to organize and deliver educational content through a single online platform. It helps reduce manual work, improves communication between instructors and students, and makes learning resources easily available.

Our goal is to provide a simple, secure, and accessible learning environment that supports modern online and distance education.
